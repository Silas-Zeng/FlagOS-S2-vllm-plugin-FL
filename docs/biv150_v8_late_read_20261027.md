## One change and one falsifier

In both full PAGE16 and masked-tail paths, move only `V = tl.load(...)` and
`value_scale = tl.load(...)` from immediately after K loading to immediately
after `running_sum`, before PV. The BF16 conversion of V was already in the PV
expression and stays there. Preserve exactly:

```python
tl.dot((probability * value_scale[None, :]).to(tl.bfloat16), V.to(tl.bfloat16))
```

No packed-i32 representation change, paired shared store, fake dependency,
constant-zero arithmetic, P8, K32, cache-format change, or production hook.
PAGE16, BF16 QK, softmax, segmentation, natural-log maxima handoff, reducer and
every original guard remain intact. Allocation stays16KiB/page; no KV capacity
is reclaimed. Shared/barrier/store layout is a preservation target, not a
claim based on moving source lines.

This is a source-scheduling falsifier. The compiler can hoist the loads/unpack
again; later loads can also lose useful latency overlap. At the initial CPU freeze, no compilation or device execution had been performed.
The owner subsequently compiled frozen3f1 (see the update below); resource
reductions still do not prove speed. PR73/4a component diagnostics are now complete
and its measured baseline dataflow was slower in all three cases; PR73 is closed.


scratch. Actual constructed shape/stride/dtype/storage_offset/contiguity must

are3.42MiB ragged,266.38MiB4K and517.19MiB16K (allocator caching may retain earlier
blocks). No initialization, cache writer, attention or reduction executes in
this mode, and no `use_sme`/ABI override is supplied.
It exports actual BF16 baseline, frozen V8 and late V8
TTGIR/LLVM/ASM/backend metadata and resources for the same specialization.
Full and tail must retain BF16 MMA, eight packed V loads,32 byte extractions,
32 i8→BF16 conversions, and place extraction/conversion **after all softmax
exp2 instructions**. The checker also compares shared bytes, V blocked/shared
layout definitions, shared-store/barrier/conversion/MMA counts against frozen V8.
Registers/spills/use_sme are reported without a speed claim. Missing debug
mapping or unfamiliar lowering fails closed for manual review. The checker is
deliberately conservative; it does not substitute for inspecting actual shared
addresses or establish latency/occupancy.
